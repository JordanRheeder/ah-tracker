      Enchanting shuffles

      Enchanting materials (dusts, essences, shards, crystals, rods and reagents like Righteous Orb and the elemental essences) and essence conversions (3 Lesser = 1 Greater: buy one, right-click to combine or split, sell the other), and what each route makes: buy at the cheapest listing now, sell after that house's AH cut (5% Alliance, 15% Neutral) and expected lost deposits. Between houses you sell at the other house's cheapest listing now; on the same house, at its usual price (7-day median). Routes that sell where nobody buys (e.g. Horde-only items on Alliance) are left out. Prices are your latest scans, or Undermine's hourly snapshot if newer.
